DotShare 2.0.0 — Smarter Social Sharing for Developers

8 November 2025 · 1 min read
#vscode#ai#opensource#freerave







I’m excited to share that DotShare v2.0.0 is now live on the Visual Studio Marketplace
!
DotShare is a VS Code extension that helps developers share project updates directly to social media — powered by AI or written manually.
What’s new in this release:
Manual post creation (no AI needed)
Drag-and-drop media uploads (images & videos)
Smarter language switching (English, Arabic, Russian)
improved Reddit integration (r/ and u/ support)
Smoother UI and bug fixes across the board

Whether you’re announcing a release, sharing your dev journey, or posting updates to multiple platforms — DotShare makes it easy right from your editor.
Try it here https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=FreeRave.dotshare
